Sportacular Analytics Methodology

The boards are ranked by Sportacular Score. This page explains that score and the numbers behind it.

Back Home View Plans

Our Approach

Sportacular Analytics ranks today's games and props by Sportacular Score. The score blends the price edge, confidence, risk, and the recent trend. Expected value, model probability, and hit rate stay on the card so you can see the pieces behind the score.

The platform is designed to help users analyze information from multiple angles rather than rely on a single number. Model outputs should be considered alongside the underlying market, player and team context, recent trends, and available data.

Sportacular Score

Sportacular Score is the 0 to 100 grade used to rank the best side of a game or a player prop. It blends the price edge, confidence, risk, and the recent trend into one number. A higher score is a stronger Sportacular play. Rows marked No Play stay off the public boards.

The score should be used as a comparison tool, not as a prediction that an outcome will win.

Hit Rate

Hit Rate shows how often the result has happened in the logged games. On a player prop, it is how often that player has cleared the posted line. On a game, it is how often the team has covered the spread or the total has gone over.

A 70% hit rate means the result landed in 7 of every 10 logged games that counted. A small sample can show a high rate, so the score also looks at confidence and risk.

Expected Value (EV)

Expected Value is a way of evaluating the potential value identified at a listed sportsbook price. Positive EV means the model identifies a price that may be more favorable than its estimated probability would suggest.

A larger positive EV can indicate a stronger potential statistical value, but positive EV does not mean an outcome is guaranteed to occur.

Model Probability

Model Probability is Sportacular's estimated probability for an analyzed outcome. For player props, it represents the estimated probability that the recommended Over or Under outcome will occur.

Model probabilities are estimates based on available data and should not be interpreted as guarantees of future results.

Price Edge

Price Edge helps describe the difference between a model-based view of an outcome and the price available in the market. It is another way to evaluate whether the listed sportsbook price may offer statistical value.

Price Edge and Expected Value are related analytical concepts, but they are displayed as distinct metrics within Sportacular Analytics.

Sportacular Edge

Sportacular Edge is an analytical edge metric used by the platform when evaluating and comparing opportunities. It is maintained separately from Expected Value and Price Edge so users can evaluate multiple dimensions of the model output.

A stronger edge represents a stronger model-identified analytical advantage, not a guaranteed outcome.

Model Confidence & Confidence Tier

Model Confidence communicates the strength of the model's confidence in an analytical output. Confidence Tier provides a simplified categorical view of confidence so users can compare opportunities more quickly.

Confidence is not the same as probability, expected value, or risk. A high-confidence model output can still be wrong, and all sports outcomes contain uncertainty.

Risk Tier

Risk Tier provides a separate view of the uncertainty or risk associated with an analytical opportunity. It is intentionally distinct from Model Confidence and Confidence Tier.

Users should consider risk together with the available probability, edge, expected value, trend, and market information rather than treating any single metric as decisive.

Player & Team Trends

Trend information provides recent statistical context for players and teams. Recent form can help explain what has been happening, but past performance does not guarantee that the same pattern will continue.

Sample size matters. Early or limited samples may provide less context than established statistical samples, which is why trends should be interpreted alongside broader data.

Sportsbook Markets & Data

Sportacular Analytics compares available sportsbook markets and sports data to help users evaluate opportunities across multiple sources. Sportsbook prices, lines, player information, statistics, and other third-party data can change over time.

Because the platform relies on external sports data and odds sources, data may occasionally contain errors, delays, missing information, or differences between providers.

How to Use the Analytics

Start with the Sportacular Score, then check the hit rate, the price, and the risk. No single number is a guaranteed result. The score is a comparison tool.

Sportacular Analytics is intended to make those signals easier to compare and understand so users can perform their own analysis.

How to Read Game Odds

Hover over any term below for a quick explanation in plain English.

Best Market
Best Market
The first row of the summary table. It names the market and side Sportacular ranks first. The card can say: Total · Over, Total · Under, Spread · Home, Spread · Away, Moneyline · Home, Moneyline · Away, or No Play.
Sportacular Score
Sportacular Score
The 0 to 100 grade in the summary table. It blends the price edge, confidence, risk, and recent trend. Higher means a stronger Sportacular play. The same grade is labeled Score on the Sportacular Scores table and the Top Sportacular Scores table. The card can say a number such as 82.4, or — when there is no play.
Confidence
Confidence
The Confidence row in the summary and the Confidence column on the Sportacular Scores table. It is not the hit rate, the EV, or the risk. The card can say: Elite, Strong, Solid, Lean, or —.
Risk
Risk
The Risk row in the summary and the Risk column on the Sportacular Scores table. A higher score can still carry more risk. The card can say: Low, Medium, High, Very High, or —.
Hit Rate
Hit Rate
The Hit Rate row in the summary. How often this result has happened in the logged games. On the Sportacular Scores table it is labeled Hit. The card can say a percent such as 70.0%, or —. Moneyline rows say — because a hit rate is not tracked there.
Spread
Spread
The margin table. Each row is a team. Line is the point spread, and Odds is the American price. A negative line is usually the favorite. The card can say a signed line such as -3.5 or +3.5, and odds such as -110 or +100. A missing price says N/A.
Moneyline
Moneyline
The outright-winner table. The columns are Team, Odds, Implied, and Vig-Free. A ranked moneyline side is labeled ML. The card can say American odds such as -150 or +130, or N/A.
Implied
Implied
The Implied column in the Moneyline table. Each team’s chance from the listed odds before the sportsbook margin is removed. The card can say a percent such as 62%, or N/A.
Vig-Free
Vig-Free
The Vig-Free column in the Moneyline table. Each team’s chance after the sportsbook margin is removed. The card can say a percent such as 58%, or N/A.
Total
Total
The Over and Under table. The columns are Side, Line, and Odds. Both sides use the same line. The Side column can say Over or Under. The card can say a line such as 224.5 and odds such as -110 or +100, or N/A.
Bet
Bet
The Bet column on the Sportacular Scores table and the Top Sportacular Scores table. The card can say Over or Under plus the total, a team name plus the spread, or a team name plus ML. The top table also shows the Game and the Book. The book can say DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars, Kalshi, PointsBet, BetRivers, or ESPN BET.
EV
EV (Expected Value)
The EV column on the Sportacular Scores table and the Top Sportacular Scores table. Positive EV means the model thinks that price is better than the chance it assigned. The card can say a plus number such as +0.25, a minus number such as -0.10, or N/A.
No Play
No Play
Shown in Best Market when no side qualifies. No Play rows stay off the Sportacular Scores table. The card can say No Play.

How to Read NFL Player Props

Hover over any term below for a quick explanation in plain English.

EV
EV (Expected Value)
A measure of how much value the model identifies at the listed price. A larger positive EV indicates a stronger potential statistical edge.
Prop Line
Prop Line
The statistical number associated with the player market, such as 249.5 passing yards or 64.5 receiving yards.
Implied Probability
Implied Probability
The approximate chance suggested by the listed sportsbook odds.
Over
Over
The selection that the player will finish above the listed statistical line.
Under
Under
The selection that the player will finish below the listed statistical line.
Sportsbook
Sportsbook
The source providing the displayed player prop line and odds.
Passing Props
Passing Props
Quarterback markets such as passing yards, completions, attempts, touchdowns, and interceptions.
Rushing and Receiving Props
Rushing and Receiving Props
Markets such as rushing yards, rushing attempts, receptions, receiving yards, and touchdowns.

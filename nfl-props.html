How to Read NFL Player Props

Hover over any term below for a quick explanation in plain English.

Best Side
Best Side
The Best Side row at the top of the card. It is the side Sportacular ranks for this prop. The card can say Over or Under. No Play rows stay off the board.
Sportacular Score
Sportacular Score
The 0 to 100 grade for the best side. It blends the price edge, confidence, risk, and recent trend. Higher means a stronger Sportacular play. The same grade is labeled Score on the top props table. The card can say a number such as 82.4, or —.
Confidence
Confidence
The Confidence row. It is not the hit rate, the EV, or the risk. The card can say Elite, Strong, Solid, Lean, a 0 to 100 number, or —.
Risk
Risk
The Risk row. It is separate from confidence. A higher Sportacular Score can still carry more risk. The card can say Low, Medium, High, Very High, or —.
Hit Rate
Hit Rate
The Hit Rate row. How often this result has happened in the logged games. The card can say a percent such as 70%, or —.
EV
EV (Expected Value)
The EV row on the card and the EV column on the top props table. Positive EV means the model thinks the price may be better than the chance it assigned. The card can say a plus number such as +0.25, a minus number such as -0.10, or N/A.
Bet Type
Bet Type
The Bet Type column in the detail table, and the Bet column on the top props table. The card can say Over or Under.
Line
Line
The Line column. The number the sportsbook set for this prop. The card can say a number such as 24.5, or N/A.
Odds
Odds
The Odds column. The American payout price for the listed side. Bigger plus-money odds pay more, but usually hit less often. The card can say a price such as -115 or +120, or N/A.
Model Probability
Model Probability
The Model Probability column. The model’s estimate of how often the listed side should hit. The card can say a percent such as 58%, or N/A.
Sportsbook
Sportsbook
The book named under the player. It is the source of the line and odds on that card, and it is labeled Book on the top props table. The card can say DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars, Kalshi, PointsBet, BetRivers, or ESPN BET.
Passing Props
Passing Props
Quarterback markets. The card title can say Passing Yards, Completions, Attempts, Passing Tds, or Interceptions.
Rushing and Receiving Props
Rushing and Receiving Props
Skill-position markets. The card title can say Rushing Yards, Rushing Attempts, Receptions, Receiving Yards, or touchdown markets.

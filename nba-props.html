How to Read Props

Hover over any term below for a quick explanation in plain English.

Best Side
Best Side
The Best Side row at the top of the card. It is the Over or Under Sportacular ranks for this prop. No Play rows stay off the board.
Sportacular Score
Sportacular Score
The 0 to 100 grade for the best side. It blends the price edge, confidence, risk, and recent trend. Higher means a stronger Sportacular play. The same grade is labeled Score on the top props table.
Confidence
Confidence
The Confidence row. It is the confidence tier for that side. It is not the hit rate, the EV, or the risk.
Risk
Risk
The Risk row. It is separate from confidence. A higher Sportacular Score can still carry more risk.
Hit Rate
Hit Rate
The Hit Rate row. How often this result has happened in the logged games. A 70% hit rate means it landed in 7 of every 10 logged games that counted.
EV
EV (Expected Value)
The EV row on the card and the EV column on the top props table. Positive EV means the model thinks the price may be better than the chance it assigned. A plus sign means positive.
Bet Type
Bet Type
The Bet Type column in the detail table, and the Bet column on the top props table. It shows whether the listed side is Over or Under.
Line
Line
The Line column. The number the sportsbook set for this prop.
Odds
Odds
The Odds column. The American payout price for the listed side. Bigger plus-money odds pay more, but usually hit less often.
Model Probability
Model Probability
The Model Probability column. The model’s estimate of how often the listed side should hit.
